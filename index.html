Questions owners ask

What am I paying for?

Two things. The equipment is a one-time purchase that you own: a gateway plus one sensor for each unit. The monthly plan pays for 24/7 monitoring, text and email alerts, your inspection logs, and support.

Is there a contract?

No long-term contract. The monthly plan can be canceled with 30 days notice. The yearly plan costs less and is paid up front.

What happens if a sensor breaks?

Equipment comes with a 1-year warranty. If a sensor fails in that time, we replace it. Night Shift also alerts you if a sensor stops reporting, so a dead battery never looks like "all clear."

Do I install it myself?

Yes, and it is simple. Every sensor arrives registered to your account. Plug in the gateway, mount each sensor outside its cooler with the probe inside, then scan or type the sensor ID on your setup page and name the unit. We walk you through it on a phone or video call if you want help.

Can I add units later?

Yes. Each added sensor is a one-time equipment cost plus the per-unit monitoring fee. Your gateway already covers the whole building.

What do I show the health inspector?

Open the logs on any phone or print them. Every reading, alert, peak temperature, and time out of range is recorded automatically.
